NAAC ACCREDITATION GUIDELINES – CRITERION 1: CURRICULAR ASPECTS
=================================================================

1.1 Curriculum Design and Development
--------------------------------------
The institution should demonstrate its commitment to curriculum design that is aligned with 
the vision and mission of the institution, regulatory guidelines, and NEP 2020 requirements.

Key Metrics (1.1.1):
- Percentage of programmes that include experiential learning
- Industry/employer involvement in curriculum design
- Number of online courses offered (MOOCs, NPTEL, Coursera)
- Percentage of courses with Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

Evidence Required:
- Minutes of BOS (Board of Studies) meetings
- Curriculum revision records (last 5 years)
- MoU copies with industries for curriculum input
- List of online courses with enrollment data
- NEP 2020 implementation plan

Best Practices for Criterion 1:
- Form an Academic Advisory Board with 40% industry representation
- Review curriculum every 2 years with graduate feedback
- Map every course to specific Programme Outcomes (POs)
- Implement outcome-based education (OBE) framework

1.2 Academic Flexibility
-------------------------
Institutions must provide academic flexibility to students including:
- Horizontal mobility between programmes
- Vertical progression pathways
- Integration of vocational courses
- Multiple entry/exit options as per NEP 2020

Metric 1.2.1: Percentage of students enrolled in subject-related Certificate/Diploma/Add-on programmes

Formula = (Number of students enrolled in Certificate/Diploma programmes / 
           Total number of students enrolled) × 100

Grading Scale:
- >50%  → 4 (Excellent)
- 40-50% → 3 (Good)
- 30-40% → 2 (Average)
- <30%  → 1 (Below Average)

1.3 Curriculum Enrichment
--------------------------
- Value-added courses should cover: professional skills, life skills, gender sensitization, 
  environmental education, human values, professional ethics
- At least 30% of courses should integrate crosscutting issues

1.4 Feedback System
--------------------
Institutions should collect feedback on curriculum from:
1. Students (current and alumni)
2. Teachers
3. Employers and industry experts
4. Academic peers

The feedback should be analyzed, and action taken reports must be documented.
