NAAC CRITERIA 4–7 QUICK REFERENCE
====================================

CRITERION 4: INFRASTRUCTURE AND LEARNING RESOURCES
---------------------------------------------------
4.1 Physical Facilities
  - Adequate classrooms, labs, sports facilities
  - Barrier-free environment for persons with disabilities
  - Green campus / energy conservation measures
  Metric 4.1.2: Expenditure for infrastructure augmentation ≥ 2 Crore/year → Grade A

4.2 Library as a Learning Resource
  - Library automation (KOHA/SOUL/LibSys)
  - e-resources: INFLIBNET/DELNET/N-LIST subscription
  - Footfall data and library usage statistics
  Metric 4.2.2: Expenditure on library resources

4.3 IT Infrastructure
  - Internet bandwidth ≥ 1 Gbps for Grade A+
  - Student computing units per student
  - Wi-Fi campus coverage
  - LMS (Learning Management System) adoption rate

4.4 Maintenance of Campus Infrastructure
  - Annual expenditure on maintenance ≥ 10% of total budget
  - Physical and academic support facilities maintained

CRITERION 5: STUDENT SUPPORT AND PROGRESSION
---------------------------------------------
5.1 Student Support
  - Government/institutional scholarships and freeships
  - Capacity building and skill enhancement programmes
  - Anti-ragging measures and student grievance cell
  - Career counseling and placement support

5.2 Student Progression
  - Progression to higher education
  - Students qualified in national-level examinations (GATE/NET/CAT)
  - Placements (campus recruitment) statistics
  - Entrepreneurship development and startups by students

5.3 Student Participation and Activities
  - Sports and cultural activities at national/international level
  - Student council and representation
  - Alumni association engagement

CRITERION 6: GOVERNANCE, LEADERSHIP & MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------
6.1 Institutional Vision and Leadership
  - Strategic Plan and deployment documents
  - Participative management through committees

6.2 Strategy Development and Deployment
  - IQAC (Internal Quality Assurance Cell) functionality
  - e-governance in administration (ERP systems)
  - Annual Quality Assurance Reports (AQARs)

6.3 Faculty Empowerment Strategies
  - Welfare schemes for faculty/staff
  - Performance appraisal system
  - Average number of training/FDP days per teacher per year

6.4 Financial Management and Resource Mobilization
  - Audited financial statements
  - Resource mobilization beyond fees
  - Utilization of funds for academic quality

CRITERION 7: INSTITUTIONAL VALUES AND BEST PRACTICES
------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Institutional Values and Social Responsibilities
  - Gender equity programmes (Women's Cell)
  - Environmental consciousness (green audit, waste management)
  - Human values and professional ethics courses
  - Divyangjan (PwD) support measures

7.2 Best Practices
  - Two best practices per cycle documented in NAAC format
  - Must show objectives, context, practice description, and evidence of success

7.3 Institutional Distinctiveness
  - Unique strengths of the institution
  - Contribution to the local community and society

NAAC GRADING SCALE (Overall CGPA)
------------------------------------
A++ → CGPA ≥ 3.76 (Accredited)
A+  → CGPA 3.51–3.75
A   → CGPA 3.01–3.50
B++ → CGPA 2.76–3.00
B+  → CGPA 2.51–2.75
B   → CGPA 2.01–2.50
C   → CGPA 1.51–2.00
D   → Accreditation Denied (CGPA < 1.51)
