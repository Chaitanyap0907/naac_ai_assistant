NAAC ACCREDITATION GUIDELINES – CRITERION 3: RESEARCH, INNOVATIONS & EXTENSION
================================================================================

3.1 Resource Mobilization for Research
----------------------------------------
Institutions should demonstrate a research culture through:
- Dedicated research promotion policy
- Seed money provided to faculty for research
- Percentage of teachers with ongoing projects
- Research facilities and laboratories

Metric 3.1.1: Grants received from Government/non-government bodies for R&D projects
Formula: Total grants (Rs. in Lakhs) / Number of teachers

Grading:
- >3 Lakhs per teacher → 4
- 2-3 Lakhs → 3
- 1-2 Lakhs → 2
- <1 Lakh → 1

3.2 Innovation Ecosystem
--------------------------
Key indicators:
- Incubation centre with number of start-ups nurtured
- Awards/recognition for innovation (Hackathons, ARIIA ranking)
- IPR policy and number of patents filed/granted
- Technology Transfer records

Metric 3.2.2: Workshops/Seminars conducted on Research Methodology/IPR/Entrepreneurship
Evidence: Event reports, participant certificates, photographs, feedback forms

3.3 Research Publications and Awards
--------------------------------------
Metric 3.3.1: Number of Ph.D. registered per eligible teacher
Metric 3.3.2: Number of research publications in peer-reviewed journals

Publication Quality Indicators:
- Scopus/Web of Science indexed publications
- H-index and citations
- Books and book chapters with ISBN
- Conference proceedings (IEEE, ACM, Springer)

3.4 Extension Activities
--------------------------
Community engagement through:
- NSS (National Service Scheme) activities
- NCC activities
- Extension and outreach programmes
- Collaboration with community/NGOs

Metric 3.4.2: Awards/recognitions received for extension activities
Metric 3.4.3: Number of extension activities conducted with government/government recognized bodies

3.5 Collaboration
------------------
MoUs and linkages with:
- National institutions (IITs, NITs, Central Universities)
- International institutions
- Industries and corporate bodies
- R&D organizations (CSIR, DRDO, ISRO, DST)

Benefits from collaborations:
- Faculty development programmes
- Student exchange programmes
- Research project collaboration
- Industry internships and placements
