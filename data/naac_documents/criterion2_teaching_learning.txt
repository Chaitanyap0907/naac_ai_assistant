NAAC ACCREDITATION GUIDELINES – CRITERION 2: TEACHING-LEARNING & EVALUATION
=============================================================================

2.1 Student Enrollment and Profile
------------------------------------
Metric 2.1.1: Demand Ratio = (Number of seats filled / Sanctioned intake) × 100

The institution must demonstrate:
- Transparent admission process
- Reservation policy adherence (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD)
- Diversity in student enrollment (state/national level)

Key Evidence:
- Admission data for past 5 years
- Reservation category-wise enrollment data
- State/UT-wise student data
- First-generation college-goers percentage

2.2 Catering to Student Diversity
-----------------------------------
Institutions should have mechanisms to:
- Identify slow and advanced learners through diagnostic tests
- Provide bridge/remedial courses for weaker students
- Offer advanced learning resources for meritorious students
- Use Student Satisfaction Survey (SSS) outcomes

Metric 2.2.1: Student-Teacher Ratio (STR)
- Ideal STR for general programmes: 25:1
- Sciences/Professional: 20:1
- Engineering: 15:1

2.3 Teaching-Learning Process
-------------------------------
Modern Pedagogical Methods to be documented:
1. Experiential Learning: field visits, internships, project-based learning
2. Participative Learning: seminars, group discussions, role-play
3. Problem-solving Methodology: case studies, design thinking workshops
4. ICT-enabled teaching: smart classrooms, LMS (Moodle/SWAYAM)
5. Flipped Classroom methodology

Metric 2.3.1: Student-centric methods used for enhancing learning experiences
Evidence: Photographs, attendance records, student reports, faculty declarations

2.4 Teacher Profile and Quality
---------------------------------
Metric 2.4.1: Percentage of full-time teachers against sanctioned posts
Metric 2.4.2: Percentage of teachers with NET/SET/SLET/Ph.D.
Metric 2.4.3: Teachers' experience in industry/institutions of higher learning

Average annual days for teachers in FDP (Faculty Development Programmes):
- ≥15 days → 4
- 10-15 days → 3
- 5-10 days → 2
- <5 days → 1

2.5 Evaluation Process and Reforms
------------------------------------
The institution should demonstrate:
- Transparency in evaluation process
- Grievance redressal mechanism for examination
- IT-enabled examination management
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) system
- Automated result processing

Metric 2.5.1: Mechanism of Internal Assessment is transparent and robust
Evidence: Examination Manual, IA marks display records, grievance records

2.6 Student Performance and Learning Outcomes
----------------------------------------------
Programme Outcomes (POs) must be:
1. Clearly defined for every programme
2. Mapped to Course Outcomes (COs)
3. Assessed through direct and indirect methods
4. Used to drive curriculum improvement (feedback loop)

Graduate Attributes expected:
- Critical thinking and problem-solving
- Communication and teamwork
- Ethical and social responsibility
- Life-long learning aptitude
